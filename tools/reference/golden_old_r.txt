Chinese history, spanning over four millennia, is a complex tapestry of political fragmentation, imperial unification, cultural synthesis, and social transformation. While often viewed through the lens of dynastic cycles—periods of rise, stability, decline, and collapse—the narrative is also defined by pivotal turning points that fundamentally altered the trajectory of East Asian civilization. This summary outlines the major dynasties and critical inflection points that shaped modern China.
